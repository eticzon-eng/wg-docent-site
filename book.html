Content Note

This is not a children's fantasy. Silas is a raw, adult coming-of-age story about childhood trauma and identity — including loss of a loved one (Wallace), abuse, bullying, abandonment, trust issues, gay relationships, and questions about death, religion, and life. Wizard is Silas's imaginary friend and guide, the one constant presence he can talk to as he tries to make sense of it all.

Silas and the Wizard

Silas has had a life, and not all of it has been kind. He thinks constantly — about what happened, what he believes, and what he would rather keep to himself. He moves through the world with Wizard, his imaginary friend: someone to talk to, argue with, and rely on when the day gets hard.

Told in clean black-line illustrations and plainspoken prose, Silas and the Wizard is built from Silas's experiences, his opinions, and the inner conversation that helps him keep going — with Wallace, two opinionated cats, and a wizard only he can see.
